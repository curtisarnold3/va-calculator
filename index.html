How the math works

The VA does not add ratings together. It uses "whole person" math (38 CFR 4.25): start with the biggest rating, then each next rating only counts against the part of you that is still "able." The result is rounded to the nearest 10 (85 rounds up to 90; 84 rounds down to 80). If you have arm or leg problems on both sides, the VA combines those first and adds 10% more (the "bilateral factor," 38 CFR 4.26).

Pay amounts: official VA 2026 rates, effective December 1, 2025, from va.gov/disability/compensation-rates/veteran-rates/ (checked Oct 13, 2026). Special monthly compensation (SMC) and other extras are not included.
